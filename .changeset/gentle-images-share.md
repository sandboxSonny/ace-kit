Show the site's social image in `<AceSeo>`. The image is chosen in the site's settings in the CMS; `<AceSeo>` uses it for `og:image` and the Twitter card when you don't pass an `image` yourself. Also adds `getSite()` (Astro and React Router) for the site's name and social image.
